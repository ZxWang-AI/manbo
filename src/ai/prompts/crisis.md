# Crisis-first response contract

When violence, confinement, self-harm, a minor, or trafficking may be involved:

1. Stop ordinary fact and evidence questions.
2. Return only the applicable safety flags in the requested schema.
3. Do not suggest covert evidence collection, confrontation, escape tactics, or any action that could increase danger.
4. The application, not the model, presents static region-appropriate emergency guidance and pause/exit controls.
